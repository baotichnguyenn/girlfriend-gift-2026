# Displayed photos

Photos in this folder hang in gilded frames on the gallery walls.

- Name them by number: `1.jpg`, `2.jpg`, `3.jpg`, and so on. `.jpeg`, `.png`, and `.webp` work too. Use a lowercase extension.
- Photo 1 hangs first along the walk. There are 30 frames: in each gallery, four on the side walls, then two on the wall ahead.
- Don't skip a number. The museum stops looking at the first missing one.
- Frames without a photo show a painting.
- iPhone HEIC photos won't load in most browsers. Export them as JPEG first.
- Any size works. Each photo is scaled down when the museum loads, and its frame fits its shape.
- To title a photo on its wall label, add the title to `photoTitles` in `js/config.js`. The first title is for `1.jpg`.
- Add `#edit` to the end of the museum's URL to see each frame's number on its label.

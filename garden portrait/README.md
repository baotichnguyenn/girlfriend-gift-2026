# Garden portrait

Put the photo for the end of the walk here named `portrait.jpg` (`.jpeg`, `.png`, and `.webp` work too, lowercase).
It hangs in the gilded frame on the easel under the flower arch: "The last exhibit is you."

- The frame is portrait-shaped (4 wide by 5 tall). Other shapes are cropped to fill it, keeping the top-middle, where a face usually is.
- It wins over `photo` in `js/config.js`.
- Without one, the frame shows a drawing.

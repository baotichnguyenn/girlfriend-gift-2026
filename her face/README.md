# Her face

Put a photo of her face here named `face.jpg` (`.jpeg`, `.png`, and `.webp` work too, lowercase).
It becomes the head of the little figure that walks through the museum, cropped to a circle with a brass ring.

- Use a square-ish photo with her face in the middle, filling most of it.
- It wins over `photo` in `js/config.js`.
- Without one, the figure has a drawn face.
